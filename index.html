FAQ

Common questions.

If you're wondering about something, chances are someone else did too.

How much does a small business website cost?

A basic website is $500 — that's the full setup price, not a starting point. For businesses that need more pages, advanced SEO, a gallery, or service area pages, the premium package starts at $800. Monthly support is $80/mo and can be cancelled anytime. You'll always know the exact price before anything starts.

Can I see a homepage preview before paying?

Yes — that's exactly how I work. Before you pay anything, I'll build a homepage preview for your business so you can see the layout and direction. If you like it, we'll finish the full site. If not, no charge.

Do you build websites for contractors and local service businesses?

Yes, that's exactly who I build for. I've built sites for auto body shops, landscapers, tint shops, home service companies, cleaning businesses, and more. Local service businesses are my specialty.

Will my website work on mobile?

Every site I build is mobile-first — meaning it's designed for phones first, then scaled up to desktop. Most of your customers will visit from their phones, so this is non-negotiable.

Do you offer monthly website support?

Yes. For $80/month, I handle security monitoring, backups, software updates, and one content update per month. No long-term contract — cancel anytime.

Can you help with SEO?

Basic on-page SEO is included in every site — title tags, meta descriptions, local keywords, image alt text, and schema markup. For businesses that want more advanced local SEO, that's available as an add-on.

Do I need a custom domain?

Your domain and hosting are included in the monthly support plan — no need to figure it out yourself. As long as you're on the $80/mo plan, it's covered. If you ever cancel, I'll help you transfer everything over so you never lose access to your site.

How long does it take to launch?

Most sites are delivered within 1–7 days of receiving your content (photos, services, business info). The free preview is typically ready within 1 hour of your initial form submission.
